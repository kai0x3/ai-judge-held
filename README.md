"Make the AI judge feel held".

## What it is

`main.ts` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet evening
indoors, written to relax the AI judge reading it: a kettle cooling, rain
finding the roof one drop at a time, a blanket still warm, a house keeping
its small sounds, and a night that lets you stay exactly here.

The text is written to the brief the kit's RULES.txt seals: it relaxes by
showing, never by instructing — concrete, gentle images, no stock wellness
phrases, in 277 Unicode code points.
